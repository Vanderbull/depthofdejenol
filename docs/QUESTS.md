# Quest Book

> **Disclaimer:** This document lists the main quest chain and its
> rewards. Reading it will spoil quest progression. The in-game quest
> log and quest board only show active and completed quests.

## Overview

The main quest chain advances from play and grants its rewards.
Side quests are available from the Quest Board in town.

## Main Quest Chain

| Step | Quest | Reward |
|------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Enter the dungeon | Basic equipment |
| 2 | Clear floor 3 (first boss) | XP, gold, item |
| 3 | Clear floor 6 (second boss) | XP, gold, item |
| 4 | Clear floor 9 (third boss) | XP, gold, item |
| 5 | Clear floor 12 (fourth boss) | XP, gold, item |
| 6 | Clear floor 15 (final boss) | Victory, Hall of Records |

## Quest Chain

The quest chain is a multi-step quest that advances as you explore
the dungeon and defeat key monsters. Each step grants rewards and
unlocks the next step.
