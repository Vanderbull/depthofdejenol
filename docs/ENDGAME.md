# Endgame Book

> **Disclaimer:** This document lists New Game Plus mechanics and
> achievements. Reading it will spoil post-game content. The in-game
> endgame only reveals what you have unlocked.

## Overview

After defeating the final boss, the player can start New Game Plus
(NG+), which carries the party and scales monster stats.

![Treasure](https://github.com/Vanderbull/depthofdejenol/blob/main/resources/images/treasure.png)

## New Game Plus

| NG+ Level | Monster Multiplier | Rewards Scale |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| NG+0 (base) | 1.0x | Normal |
| NG+1 | 1.5x | +50% |
| NG+2 | 2.0x | +100% |
| NG+3 | 2.5x | +150% |

## What Carries Over

- Party members and their levels
- Equipment and items
- Gold and bank storage
- Bestiary and journal progress
- Guild memberships

## What Resets

- Dungeon progress (you start from floor 1)
- Quest progress (you re-do the main quest)
- Achievements (you re-earn them)

## The Abyss

The Abyss is a mega-dungeon available in NG+ that offers the
hardest challenges and best rewards in the game.

## Arena

The Arena lets you fight monsters for practice and rewards.
You can bet gold on the outcome.

## Achievements

There are 25 achievements that unlock and persist across saves.
They track milestones like:
- Defeating bosses
- Reaching max level
- Completing the quest chain
- Winning Arena fights
- Finding rare items
- Surviving NG+ cycles
