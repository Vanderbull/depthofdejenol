# Town Book

> **Disclaimer:** This document lists all town services and what they
> do. Reading it will spoil town exploration. The in-game town
> only shows buildings as you discover them.

## Overview

The town is the hub between dungeon runs. It offers services for
healing, shopping, banking, guild management, and more.

![Tavern](https://github.com/Vanderbull/depthofdejenol/blob/main/resources/images/tavern.png)

## Gold Sinks

Gold is spent on:
- Resurrection at the Morgue
- Identification and uncursing at the General Store
- Guild leveling and spell learning
- Rest and healing at the Tavern
- Divination at the Seer
- Cures for disease, poison, paralysis

## Town Progression

As the party levels up and completes quests, new services become
available and existing services offer better stock.
