# Lifecycle Book

> **Disclaimer:** This document lists aging, marriage, and family
> mechanics. Reading it will spoil character progression. The in-game
> character sheet and events show aging as it happens.

## Overview

Characters age over time with race-specific decay and max age.
Coming-of-age happens at 18. Characters can marry and have children.

![NPC](resources/images/npc.png)

## Aging

- Characters age 1 year per in-game time period
- Stats decay with age (race-specific rates)
- Maximum age is race-specific (100-400 years)
- Death by old age is possible
- The body reaches the Morgue on death

## Coming of Age

- At age 18, characters become adults
- Adults can marry, have children, and retire
- Retirement eligibility at age 70

## Marriage

- Characters can marry in town
- Spouse is tracked in the character sheet
- Marriage is permanent

## Children and Family

- Married couples can have children
- Children are tracked in the family lineage
- Descendants can inherit from ancestors
- Family data persists in JSON saves

## Retirement

- At age 70, characters become eligible for retirement
- Retired characters stop aging
- They can still be played but gain no further stat growth
