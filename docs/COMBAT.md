# Combat Book

> **Disclaimer:** This document lists combat mechanics, status effects,
> and monster abilities. Reading it will spoil tactical surprises.
> The in-game combat log shows what happens in real time.

## Overview

Combat is tactical: pick targets, see each monster, use front/back
rows, and manage spell selection.

## Combat Flow

1. Encounter starts — monsters are displayed
2. Party members act in initiative order (dexterity-based)
3. Each action: attack, cast spell, use item, or defend
4. Monsters act after the party
5. Combat ends when one side is defeated

## Front/Back Row

- **Front row** — takes full damage, deals full damage
- **Back row** — takes reduced damage, deals reduced damage
- Melee attacks can only target the front row
- Spells can target any row

## Status Effects

| Effect | Cause | Cure |
|--------|-------|------|
| Poison | Spiders, snakes, scorpions | Cure Poison, Tavern |
| Blind | Certain monsters | Rest, Tavern |
| Confusion | Mind spells, certain monsters | Rest, Tavern |
| Fire | Fire-breathing monsters | Rest, Tavern |
| Paralysis | Certain monsters | Cure Paralysis, Tavern |

## Monster Abilities

- **Poison** — spiders, snakes, scorpions, centipedes
- **Fire breath** — dragons, demons, devils, fire monsters
- **Regeneration** — trolls, oozes, slimes
- **Spellcasting** — mages, wizards, sorcerers, enchanters

## Damage Calculation

Damage is based on:
- Weapon attack bonus
- Character strength
- Character level
- Monster defense
- Hit chance (dexterity vs. level)

## XP and Loot

After combat, the party receives:
- XP based on monster level
- Gold based on monster level and floor
- Items from monster drops
