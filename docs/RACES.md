## Descriptions

- **Human**: Humans are the most common race in Mordor. They possess average abilities and are able to excel in any guild or class.
- **Dwarf**: Dwarves are a strong and hearty race, native to underground tunnels and caverns. They are natural warriors with a strong resistance to poisons.
- **Elf**: Elves are an agile and intelligent race. They excel at magic and are very dexterous, but they are also physically weak.
- **Gnome**: Gnomes are a small, playful, and intelligent race with strong magical abilities. They are known to be physically weak but very wise.
- **Hobbit**: Hobbits are small and charming creatures. Their small stature allows them to sneak around unnoticed. They have a natural ability to escape from danger, but they lack strength and magical abilities.
- **Half-Orc**: Half-Orcs are a very strong, yet unintelligent race. They are the most powerful warriors in the land, but they lack magical ability and charisma.
